If you've ever stood in the produce aisle staring at a pile of small green fruits wrapped in papery husks, wondering what to do with them, this guide is for you. Tomatillo recipes have a reputation for being restaurant-only territory, but they're actually some of the easiest things you'll ever cook. Tart, citrusy, and a little sticky under that husk, tomatillos are the backbone of salsa verde and about seventeen other things you didn't know you needed.

This post covers 18 tomatillo recipes you can make with basic ingredients and one non-negotiable step: roasting. Skip that step and you'll wonder why everyone's so obsessed. Do it right and you'll get it immediately.

## **What Are Tomatillos, and Why Roast Them First**

Tomatillos look like small green tomatoes but they're actually related to the gooseberry, and they show up husked in most grocery stores. Raw, they're tart to the point of being almost unpleasant. Roasted, they turn sweet, smoky, and deeply savory. It's not a small difference. It's the difference between a flat sauce and one people ask you to bottle.

#### **How to roast tomatillos:**

* Peel off the husks and rinse well. They're sticky underneath, so don't skip the rinse.
* Cut out the stems.
* Roast at 400°F for about 15 minutes, until they wrinkle and turn an army green color.

That's it. Once you've got a batch roasted, you're ready for any of the recipes below.

### **18 Tomatillo Recipes to Try This Week**

#### **1. Homemade Salsa Verde**

This is where every tomatillo recipe collection has to start. Blend roasted tomatillos with garlic, onion, cilantro, and a chili of your choice. It beats anything from a jar, and it takes ten minutes.

**Ingredients**: 1 lb roasted tomatillos, 2 cloves garlic, ¼ cup white onion, ¼ cup cilantro, 1 serrano or jalapeño, salt to taste.

**Instructions**: Blend everything until mostly smooth. Taste, adjust salt, and swap the serrano for jalapeño if you want less heat.

#### **2. Enchiladas Verdes**

Green sauce enchiladas that put your favorite Mexican restaurant on notice. Add a splash of cream to your salsa verde for a richer, silkier sauce.

**Ingredients**: 8 corn tortillas, 2 cups shredded chicken, 1 ½ cups salsa verde, ¼ cup cream, 1 cup shredded cheese.

**Instructions**: Stir cream into salsa verde. Fill tortillas with chicken, roll, place seam-side down in a baking dish, cover with sauce and cheese, and bake at 375°F until bubbling, about 20 minutes.

#### **3. Salsa Verde Breakfast Tacos**

Scrambled eggs, crispy bacon, and salsa verde on warm tortillas. Not fancy. Just the kind of breakfast that gets you out of bed.

**Ingredients**: 4 eggs, 4 strips bacon, 4 small tortillas, ½ cup salsa verde, cotija cheese optional.

**Instructions**: Cook bacon until crisp, scramble eggs in the rendered fat, and pile everything onto warm tortillas with a spoonful of salsa verde.

#### **4. Pickled Tomatillos**

Tangy, crunchy, and dangerously easy to keep eating straight from the jar. Great on tacos, tostadas, or honestly on pizza.

**Ingredients**: 1 lb raw tomatillos (sliced), 1 cup vinegar, 1 cup water, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp salt, garlic and peppercorns to taste.

**Instructions**: Simmer vinegar, water, sugar, and salt until dissolved. Pour over sliced tomatillos in a jar and refrigerate for at least 24 hours.

#### **5. Chicken Tinga with Tomatillos**

A twist on the classic tinga, using both tomatoes and tomatillos for a bright, tangy base that balances the smokiness of chipotle.

**Ingredients**: 2 cups shredded chicken, 1 cup roasted tomatillos, 1 cup tomatoes, 2 chipotles in adobo, ½ onion, garlic.

**Instructions**: Blend tomatillos, tomatoes, chipotles, onion, and garlic. Simmer the sauce, add shredded chicken, and cook until warmed through.

#### **6. Chili Verde**

Comforting, low-effort, and built for a slow simmer. Pork shoulder is the classic choice here, but it works with whatever protein you've got on hand.

**Ingredients**: 2 lbs pork shoulder, 3 cups roasted tomatillos, 1 onion, 2 poblano peppers, garlic, cumin.

**Instructions**: Sear the pork, blend the tomatillos with onion, poblano, and garlic, and simmer everything together for 2 to 3 hours until the meat falls apart. Make a big batch. The leftovers turn into burritos and quesadillas without any extra effort.

#### **7. Creamy Avocado Salsa Verde**

Blend an avocado into your salsa verde and it becomes something else entirely. Rich, cooling, and dangerously good on burritos.

**Ingredients**: 1 lb roasted tomatillos, 1 avocado, garlic, cilantro, lime juice, salt.

**Instructions**: Blend everything until smooth and creamy. Thin with water if it's too thick to pour.

#### **8. Migas with Salsa Verde**

A Mexican breakfast staple built on crispy corn tortilla strips and scrambled eggs. A generous spoonful of salsa verde on top turns it into a flavor-packed way to start the day.

**Ingredients**: 4 eggs, 2 corn tortillas (cut into strips and fried), ½ cup salsa verde, cheese, onion.

**Instructions**: Fry tortilla strips until crisp, scramble in eggs and onion, and top with salsa verde and cheese.

#### **9. Three-Ingredient Tomatillo Chipotle Salsa**

Proof that you don't need a long ingredient list to get big flavor. Smoky, spicy, and stupidly simple.

**Ingredients**: 1 lb roasted tomatillos, 2 cloves garlic, 2 chipotles in adobo.

**Instructions**: Blend all three until smooth. Salt to taste. That's the whole recipe.

#### **10. Pozole Verde**

A hearty soup built around roasted poblanos and tomatillos, simmered into a broth that's warming without being heavy.

**Ingredients**: 2 lbs pork or chicken, 2 poblanos, 2 cups tomatillos, hominy, garlic, oregano.

**Instructions**: Roast the poblanos and tomatillos, blend into a sauce, and simmer with the meat and hominy until everything is tender.

#### **11. Roasted Tomato and Tomatillo Salsa**

Combining tomatoes and tomatillos creates a flavor profile you won't get from either one alone. Serve it chilled for an extra pop.

**Ingredients**: 1 cup roasted tomatoes, 1 cup roasted tomatillos, garlic, onion, cilantro, lime.

**Instructions**: Blend everything together and chill for at least an hour before serving.

#### **12. Chicken Burritos with Avocado Salsa Verde**

Once you've got the creamy avocado salsa verde from recipe 7 on hand, everything else in this burrito is just a bonus.

**Ingredients**: Tortillas, shredded chicken, rice, beans, avocado salsa verde, cheese.

**Instructions**: Layer everything in a warm tortilla, roll tightly, and get creative with whatever fillings you've got.

#### **13. Huevos Divorciados**

Eggs topped with both red salsa and salsa verde, for when you and your partner can't agree on which one is better.

**Ingredients**: 4 eggs, salsa verde, tomato jalapeño salsa, tortillas, queso fresco.

**Instructions**: Fry the eggs, place on tortillas, and top one half with red salsa and the other with salsa verde.

#### **14. Chicken Chili Verde over Tostadas**

A quick, weeknight-friendly version of chili verde, best served spooned over crispy tostadas.

**Ingredients**: 2 cups shredded chicken, 2 cups salsa verde, tostada shells, cotija cheese, cilantro.

**Instructions**: Simmer shredded chicken in salsa verde until heated through, then pile onto crispy tostadas.

#### **15. Chili Verde with Rice and Beans**

The same chili verde sauce, paired down to its simplest form. When the sauce is this flavorful, you don't need much else.

**Ingredients**: Chili verde sauce, cooked rice, black beans, cilantro, lime.

**Instructions**: Spoon chili verde over rice and beans. Finish with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lime.

#### **16. Enchilada Sauce from Salsa Verde**

Thin out your salsa verde with a bit of stock and a pinch of Mexican oregano, and you've got an enchilada sauce that tastes like it took hours.

**Ingredients**: 2 cups salsa verde, ½ cup chicken stock, pinch of Mexican oregano.

**Instructions**: Whisk stock and oregano into the salsa verde and simmer for 5 minutes until slightly thickened.

#### **17. Quesadillas with Avocado Salsa Verde Dip**

Crispy quesadillas dipped into the avocado salsa verde from recipe 7. Chicken is the classic filling, but the dip is really the star.

**Ingredients**: Tortillas, shredded cheese, cooked chicken, avocado salsa verde for dipping.

**Instructions**: Fill and fold tortillas, cook until crispy and golden, and serve alongside the dip.

#### **18. Carnitas with Salsa Verde**

Authentic, oven-cooked carnitas, finished with a generous spoonful of homemade salsa verde. This is the recipe that closes out the list because it deserves the spotlight.

**Ingredients**: 3 lbs pork shoulder, orange juice, garlic, cumin, bay leaves, salsa verde for serving.

**Instructions**: Braise the pork low and slow until tender, shred, then crisp under the broiler. Top with salsa verde before serving.

### **Conclusion**

Eighteen tomatillo recipes, and every single one traces back to the same roasted base. That's not a coincidence. It's the whole point. Once you've got roasted tomatillos and a solid salsa verde in your fridge, breakfast tacos, enchiladas, burritos, and soup are all just a few steps away.

You don't need a specialty grocery trip or a complicated technique to pull this off. You need one sheet pan, one blender, and about 20 minutes. That's a low bar for food that tastes this good.
